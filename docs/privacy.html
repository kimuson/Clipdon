Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

Clipdon does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything stays on your Mac.

What Clipdon stores

  • Clipdon keeps a history of items you copy, plus any favorites you save, only on your own Mac (in the app's container / Application Support folder).
  • This data never leaves your device. Clipdon has no servers and makes no network connections with your clipboard data.

What Clipdon does not do

  • No data collection and no analytics.
  • No tracking, advertising, or device identifiers.
  • No accounts, sign-in, or cloud sync.
  • No sharing of any data with third parties.

Sensitive content

  • Content that other apps mark as concealed or transient — for example, passwords from password managers — is automatically excluded from history and never stored.

Your control

  • You can clear your whole history or remove individual items at any time from within the app.
  • Removing the app deletes the data it stored on your Mac.

Children's privacy

Clipdon does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
